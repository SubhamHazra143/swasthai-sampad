A WIDE RANGE OF INVESTIGATIONS

What tests can you ask us about?

Suraksha Diagnostic’s wider network covers the categories below. The exact test, centre, slot and preparation requirements must be confirmed for your booking.

01

Blood Tests & Pathology

Routine blood, urine and laboratory investigations for health checks and doctor-advised follow-up.

  • CBC / Complete Blood Count
  • Blood sugar: fasting, PP and HbA1c
  • Lipid profile / cholesterol
  • Liver and kidney function tests
  • Thyroid profile and vitamin tests
  • Urine, stool and other laboratory tests
  • Infection-related and specialised pathology tests
Book a blood test
02

MRI Scan

MRI investigations for different parts of the body, as advised by your doctor. Suraksha lists a 1.5T silent MRI service at its Serampore centre.

  • Brain and spine MRI
  • Joint and musculoskeletal MRI
  • Other doctor-prescribed MRI studies
  • Contrast studies, where prescribed and available
Ask about MRI
03

CT Scan

CT imaging for doctor-recommended diagnostic evaluation. The specific scan and preparation depend on the referral.

  • Head, chest and abdominal CT
  • Sinus and other region-specific CT
  • Contrast CT, when prescribed
  • Advanced and cardiac CT options at designated centres
Ask about CT scan
04

USG / Ultrasound

Ultrasound examinations for different organs and clinical needs, based on the prescribed study and centre facilities.

  • Whole abdomen and lower abdomen USG
  • Pelvic and pregnancy-related scans
  • Thyroid and other organ ultrasound
  • Doppler and advanced ultrasound, where available
  • 3D/4D/5D studies at applicable centres
Ask about USG
05

X-Ray & Bone Imaging

Digital X-ray and related imaging services, depending on the body part and referral.

  • Chest X-ray
  • Bone, joint and limb X-ray
  • Spine X-ray
  • OPG (dental/jaw panoramic imaging)
  • DEXA bone-density scan, where available
Ask about X-ray
06

Heart & Cardiology Tests

Tests used to support the assessment of heart function, as advised by the treating clinician.

  • ECG
  • 2D Echo / Echocardiography
  • TMT (Treadmill Test)
  • Other cardiology investigations, where available
Ask about heart tests

Neurology Tests

Investigations that may be recommended for nerve, muscle or brain activity assessment.

  • Other specialised neurology studies, where available

Gastroenterology Procedures

Selected digestive-system investigations and procedures, subject to specialist advice and centre availability.

  • Colonoscopy
  • Sigmoidoscopy
  • Other gastroenterology procedures, where available

Suraksha Diagnostic — Serampore centre details
Do I have to visit Swasthai Sampad to pre-book?

No. Start by messaging us on WhatsApp. We’ll guide you through the booking request and confirm whether any additional centre visit or appointment is needed.

Can I get a sample collected at home?

Home collection can be requested for eligible tests. Availability depends on your PIN code, the sample required and the provider’s schedule. It does not apply to scans such as MRI, CT or X-ray.

Can I get a discount on diagnostic tests?

Ask us about current offers on eligible tests and health packages. We’ll confirm the applicable price and terms before you decide to proceed; a particular discount is not guaranteed for every test.

Are all tests performed at the Swasthai Sampad collection point?

No. Blood/sample collection and advanced imaging are different services. MRI, CT, X-ray, endoscopy and other facility-based procedures may require a visit to a designated Suraksha Diagnostic centre. We’ll confirm the appropriate location for your test.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

Share your name, contact number, test name or prescription, preferred date and PIN code if you need home collection. Please wait for us to confirm instructions, price and availability.
